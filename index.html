01 · The problem

Deals die in the gap between yes and sent.

Field reps close the job verbally, hear "sounds good, send me something," then lose the evening to paperwork and lose the deal to delay. Incumbents ignore the moment entirely.

INCUMBENTS

PandaDoc, Qwilr, Proposify

Desk-first suites bought by procurement. E-sign, CRM sync, templates. None of it works from a truck ninety seconds after the meeting.

DEFAULT BEHAVIOR

Paste notes into ChatGPT

Free drafting, but no branding, no pricing tables, no tracking, no send workflow. Reps will not trust-send raw AI output anyway.

THE COST

Two hours tonight, deal lost tomorrow

Speed-to-proposal decides commissions. Whoever sends a real proposal today wins the signature, not whoever sends Thursday.

02 · For these users

Solo closers with no desk and no boss.

We target reps who own the whole sale: estimate, close, paper, collect. No manager exists to ban AI-assisted drafting, and every hour of paperwork is an hour not selling.

HVAC estimatorsSolar surveyorsRoofersCommercial paintersMed-device repsInsurance agentsSolo consultants
03 · The feature set

Seven pieces. Nothing more ships in v1.

F1

Capture

Hold-to-talk voice note or typed bullets plus four fields: client, job type, budget range, timeline.

F2

Generate

AI structures notes into cover, approach, itemized pricing, timeline, next steps. Under sixty seconds end to end. Industry templates included.

F3

Trust-edit layer

Tap any line to rewrite tone, adjust prices, attach photos. Reps review every word before send, ten times faster than Word.

F4

Deliver, hosted first

Branded share link shows opens and which sections held attention longest. PDF export stays secondary.

F5

Follow-up engine

Day-three nudge drafted from viewing behavior and delivered by push. Follow-up speed decides signatures.

F6

Paywall

Free: two proposals monthly, watermarked. Pro at $14.99/month via RevenueCat, seven-day trial, unlimited and unbranded.

F7

Referral hook

"Made with DealSprint" footer on free documents lands mid-pitch in front of exactly one more seller.

CUT LIST

Explicitly out of v1

E-signature, CRM sync, team seats, web app, Android, offline mode. Scope discipline is the survival skill here.

OUT OF AUDIENCE

Sales orgs, deliberately

Procurement cycles run 60 to 120 days. We do not have them. Org buyers come after product-market proof.

05 · Validation

Four-agent council. Two hearings. One honest verdict.

VERDICT: FIX FIRST · judged twice on Aug 24

Believer

Nobody owns the truck moment. Whisper-class speech recognition plus structured LLM extraction finally makes rambling voice notes into dependable priced documents.

Skeptic

Reps adopt nothing without trust, orgs ban AI pricing, ChatGPT reproduces half of it for free. Dwell analytics dies if the document leaves as a raw PDF.

Investor

Realistic pre-deadline ceiling is category money, around $225 to $675 MRR. Grand-prize traction needs breakout growth we cannot assume. Prove willingness to pay first.

Proven

  • The pain: same-day turnaround wins deals
  • The wedge: mobile-only field workers unserved by desk-first incumbents
  • Tech feasibility crossed the quality bar
  • Contest fit: category awards pay craft and journey without breakout traction

Unproven

  • Willingness to pay $14.99 monthly
  • Usage frequency high enough for retention stories
  • Whether AI-drafted pricing survives the rep's own review
